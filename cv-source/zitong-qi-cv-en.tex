\entry{ACVF | Clinical Language Alignment for Frozen Brain MRI Encoders}{Submitted to ICASSP 2027}
\researchmeta{\textbf{Co-first author (listed first)} | Independently designed, implemented and evaluated the method}
\begin{itemize}
\item Designed \textbf{ACVF} to fuse frozen 3D MRI features, AAL regional volumes and BioClinicalBERT text in a 256-D space using \textbf{subject-level SigLIP alignment}. Excluded target-related inputs and evaluated backbone/modality ablations on \textbf{1,077 ADNI participants} without retraining pretrained backbones.
\item With cognitive scores excluded from AD diagnosis inputs, mean AUC improved by \textbf{0.144} across three backbones. BrainIAC AD accuracy rose \textbf{64.6\%$\rightarrow$84.3\%}, AD AUC \textbf{0.732$\rightarrow$0.922}, and sex-classification AUC \textbf{0.637$\rightarrow$0.845}; AnatCL brain-age MAE fell \textbf{4.924$\rightarrow$4.373 years}.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{3pt}
\entry{PairSelect | Training-Free Token Compression for Weakly Supervised VAD}{Submitted to ICASSP 2027}
\researchmeta{\textbf{Co-first author (listed first)} | Independently designed, implemented and evaluated the method}
\begin{itemize}
\item Reduced encoding cost through \textbf{local pairing, norm-based ranking and group quotas} after Transformer block 6, preserving token vectors and temporal input coverage. Adapted selection to VideoMAEv2, VideoMAE, TimeSformer and CLIP systems; inserting compression and switching budgets require \textbf{no retraining}.
\item At \textbf{40\% token deletion}, achieved \textbf{1.22--1.28$\times$ encoder speedup} at batch size 32. VideoMAEv2 UCF-Crime AUC rose \textbf{80.63\%$\rightarrow$81.02\%}; TimeSformer XD-Violence PR-AUC rose \textbf{76.69\%$\rightarrow$77.37\%}. At 60\% deletion, encoder speedup reached \textbf{1.47$\times$}.
\end{itemize}
